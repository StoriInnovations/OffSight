Support

OffSight is free, open-source software provided “as is”, without warranty or guaranteed support. There is no support team, no response time and no commitment to fix any particular problem. Please use it on that basis.

Help yourself first

The OffSight Help page covers setup, choosing OffSight Camera in each meeting app, everyday use, settings and troubleshooting. The same page opens from OffSight Help in the app’s menu.

The most common fixes:

  • OffSight Camera isn’t listed in a meeting app: check the OffSight menu says Camera extension: Connected, then quit and reopen the meeting app.
  • “Installed but not running”: restart your Mac. macOS sometimes waits for a restart before starting an updated camera extension.
  • No prompt when a call starts: the app is using your real camera. Choose OffSight Camera in that app’s video settings.

Report a problem

You can report bugs and suggest improvements on GitHub Issues. Reports are read when time allows; there is no guarantee of a reply or a fix. To make a report useful, include:

  • your macOS version and Mac model;
  • the meeting app and its version;
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened;
  • the output of Settings › Advanced › Copy Diagnostics. Read it before you post: it lists the names of apps that were using the camera.

Please don’t post personal information, screenshots of other people, or anything you wouldn’t want public: GitHub issues are public.

Security issues

If you find a way for a meeting app to get live video without your approval, or any other security weakness, please report it privately through GitHub’s private vulnerability reporting rather than in a public issue. See SECURITY.md.
